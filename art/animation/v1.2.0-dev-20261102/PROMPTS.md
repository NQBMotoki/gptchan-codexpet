## 跳跃与扇翼

参考图 1：`../v1.2.0-dev/sources/jump-canonical-direct.png`；参考图 2：`../../samples/claude-style-study-20261027/03-compact-revision.png`。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset: production-source keyframes for an existing desktop pet jump animation. Image 1 is the exact existing five-pose jump sheet to edit; image 2 is the definitive character identity. Preserve image 1's five full-body poses in the same layout: three poses across the top, two across the bottom, and the bottom-right sixth cell empty. Preserve the exact character, face shape, eyes, hair, horn shape, expression, dress, hands, legs, scaled tail, facial proportions, scale, costumes and cool silver-white-lavender palette in every frame. White rose head ornament and its tassel remain on viewer-right in all poses. Do not make her rounder, more chibi, glossy, or add ornaments. Keep the original intended grounded -> rising -> airborne peak -> descending -> grounded body movement. PRIMARY EDIT: animate her TWO BATLIKE DRAGON WINGS, making a single gentle, clear wingbeat during the existing jump. Each remains physically attached symmetrically behind her shoulders with the same lavender membranes, batlike scalloped wing edges and consistent anatomy. Top-left frame 1: neutral relaxed extended wings, matching original idle silhouette. Top-middle frame 2: both wings pivot noticeably UPWARD as she starts to rise; wing tips higher than roots. Top-right frame 3: both wings reach a clearly visible lifted V/upstroke near the airborne peak, with their outer tips high, membranes stretched and no new wings. Bottom-left frame 4: both wings sweep DOWN noticeably as she descends, outer tips angled lower toward waist level, membranes still visible and open. Bottom-middle frame 5: wings return to the neutral spread of frame 1 as she settles. Make the upstroke and downstroke visibly different at 192x208, with no abrupt reversal or detached wings. The face, torso and costume should not otherwise morph or change size. No particle effects, feathers, magic, background objects, labels, borders or text. Exactly solid opaque bright chroma-key green #00FF00 background, no floor or shadow. Leave generous green margins so no horn, wingtip, tail or clothing is clipped or overlaps a neighboring cell. Maintain clean, lightly pixelated sprite rendering.
```

## 工作与魔法阵光效

参考图 1：`../v1.2.0-dev/sources/work-canonical-gesture.png`；参考图 2：`../../samples/work-vfx-study-20261101/01-magic-circle-effect-concept.png`；参考图 3：根本形象。

```text
Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset: six sequential production-source keyframes for GPT-chan's working animation. Image 1 is the exact existing 3x2 frame sheet to edit and preserve. Image 2 shows the user-approved effect direction ONLY. Image 3 is the definitive character identity. Keep all six figures in exactly the existing 3-column by 2-row layout, preserve their same positions, sizes, poses, original head/body proportions, eyes, faces, hair, ivory horns, white rose hair ornament with matching tassels on viewer-right, arms, white and lavender dress, silver decorations, lavender crystals, wings and long scaled tail. Do not create a different, rounder or glossier character. Retain her relaxed upper hand stroking the small floating horizontal magic circle while her lower hand supports it and her gaze follows the circle. ONLY edit the array and its immediate vicinity: strengthen the luminous white and pale-violet concentric rings, add a compact halo hugging the rim, one small curved translucent white-violet ribbon rising visibly FROM the circle's surface, a delicate fingertip ripple and two or three tiny light motes emerging FROM its edge then fading nearby. The magic circle itself is visibly the source of the glow and motes. Vary the glow and ribbon in small coherent steps across the six images, gently building and easing back for a seamless loop. Do not scatter stars independently around wings, dress or tail. Keep the effect contained near the hands and body, and make its contours readable even when reduced to a 192x208 sprite and seen on a light background; use a thin soft lavender outline around the white light. No attacking, projectile, explosion, large beams, weapons, books, new accessories or changes to body/clothing. Preserve the restrained lightly pixelated rendering in image 1. Exactly solid opaque bright chroma-key green #00FF00 background, no shadows, text, borders, labels or watermark. Each whole character, all wingtips, horns, tail and shoes fully visible with green margins between the six cells.
```
